Help & Support
Everything you need to know about Vortex.
Quick answers
How does Vortex preserve my resume template?

Vortex attempts to modify your original resume in place. PDF preservation works best on text-based (non-scanned) PDFs with standard layouts. For complex designs, DOCX uploads offer the highest fidelity. Complex PDFs may be reconstructed to preserve readability — you will always be able to preview before downloading.

Will Vortex invent skills or experience?

No. Vortex only rewrites and re-emphasizes your actual background. Newly acquired skills you add via Skills Enrichment can appear in your skills section — but not in work experience unless you supply supporting project or experience data.

Is my resume data shared with anyone?

Your documents are stored privately and processed by the AI provider only to generate your tailored resumes and cover letters. They are never shown to other users. Only you and the CEO (for support and moderation) can access your documents.

Can I use one account for multiple candidates?

Yes. Create a separate Candidate Profile per role (e.g., Software Engineer, Data Analyst). Tailoring operations are always associated with exactly one profile, and information from different profiles is never mixed.
